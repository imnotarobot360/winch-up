# Volunteer recruitment

Copy for the two Facebook groups: *Texas Off-Road Recovery* (~6.8K) and *Houston Area Off-Road
Recovery*.

> **Do not post this until phone sign-up works.** `/join` verifies by SMS, which needs Twilio and
> an approved A2P 10DLC campaign. Until then a volunteer reaches the code screen and no code
> arrives. Posting early spends the one launch announcement you get on a broken link, in front of
> the exact people whose goodwill this depends on.

Two other things to have in place first, because the post promises them:

- **An admin watching approvals.** People sign up expecting to be approved. A week of silence
  reads as abandoned.
- **`ADMIN_ALERT_PHONES` set**, so an unmatched request reaches a person rather than nothing.

---

## 1. Message to the group admins first

These groups have rules about links and self-promotion, and the admins are the reason the current
system works at all. Ask before posting.

> Hey — I'm one of the members here. Over the last few weeks I built a small tool for the recovery
> posts and I'd like your blessing before putting it in front of the group.
>
> It doesn't replace the group. It does the part that's awkward here: when someone posts that
> they're stuck, it texts the members nearest them who have the right gear, and the first one to
> reply gets their number and location. Everyone else gets "already covered".
>
> It's free, nobody pays anybody, and there's no business behind it. I'm not selling anything and
> there are no ads.
>
> Happy to walk you through it first, or give you admin access so you can see the queue and
> approve volunteers yourselves. If you'd rather I didn't post it, that's fine too.

## 2. The post

> **We keep doing this the hard way.**
>
> Somebody posts at 9pm that they're buried to the frame off [local road]. Forty people see it.
> Six comment. Two of them are ninety minutes away. The guy with the winch three miles up the road
> is asleep and doesn't see the post until morning.
>
> I built something for that part. It doesn't replace this group — it just handles the finding.
>
> **How it works:** someone stuck fills in one page — where they are, what they're driving, how
> they're stuck. It texts the members nearest them who have the gear for it. First person to reply
> takes it, and gets their name, number and exact location. Everyone else gets a text saying it's
> covered, so nobody drives out for nothing.
>
> **What signing up means for you:**
> - You get a text only when someone near you is stuck and you've got the right equipment.
> - You reply 1 to take it or 2 to pass. Passing costs you nothing and doesn't count against you.
> - You set your own radius — 15, 30 or 60 miles — and whether you're up for night calls.
> - You can pause any time. One tap. Going on holiday, truck's in the shop, whatever.
> - Your phone number is never shown publicly. It only goes to the person you agreed to help.
>
> **Things worth saying plainly:**
>
> No money changes hands, ever. If someone asks you to pay, or a volunteer asks a stuck driver for
> cash, report it in the app and they're done.
>
> Nobody is obligated. This isn't a rota and there's no score. If you never take a job, nothing
> happens.
>
> This is not a tow service and it's not an emergency service. If someone's hurt, in water, or in
> a traffic lane, that's 911, not us.
>
> It's free. There's no company behind it, no ads, and nothing to install — it's a web page.
>
> **It's new, and it's useless until enough of us are on it.** With ten volunteers it covers
> nothing. With a hundred, somebody stuck outside Bastrop at dusk gets a text out to the three
> people actually near them. That's the whole idea.
>
> Sign up here: **https://winch-up.com/join** — takes about two minutes. An admin approves you
> before you get any texts, so scammers and tow companies can't sign up as volunteers.
>
> Works in Spanish too — the whole thing, not just the front page.
>
> Ask me anything below. If you think this is a bad idea, say that too — I'd rather hear it now.

## 3. Shorter version, for comments and DMs

## Notes on what this copy does and does not do

- **It does not claim anyone will come.** "Nobody is obligated" is in the post because it is true,
  and because the first time somebody waits two hours for a volunteer who never arrives, the
  group will remember whether the post overpromised.
- **It leads with the 9pm scenario, not the product.** Everyone in those groups has watched that
  exact thread.
- **It invites disagreement.** A tool dropped into an established group without that line reads
  as someone monetising the group's goodwill, which is the first thing people will suspect.
- **It says "no money, no ads, no company" early**, because that is the first question and the
  honest answer is currently yes. Revisit this copy if advertising (Phase 10) ships — leaving the
  line in once ads exist would be a lie the group would rightly hold against it.
- **The link is winch-up.com**, which is not registered yet. Use the Vercel URL or register the
  domain before posting.
